Privacy and security

In short: this site has no cookies, ads or visitor tracking code. The only personal data is what you write in the contact form, and it is used only to reply to you.

Who runs this site?

Turan Tech is an independent research lab founded by Ahmet Turan Taşdelen. I run this site and handle the data described below. You can reach me through the contact form.

Contact form

When you submit the form, the name, email address and message you enter are forwarded to my inbox through the formsubmit.co form service. I use them only to reply to you; I do not share or sell them, and I do not add you to any mailing list. Your message stays in my inbox for as long as our conversation needs it; just write to me if you want it deleted. formsubmit.co processes the message under its own terms while delivering it.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

The site has no cookies and no code that counts visits, measures behaviour or shows ads. All files, including fonts, are served from this site; opening a page does not make your browser contact third parties such as Google.

Two exceptions you should know about:

  • Hosting: the site runs on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses to operate and secure the service; these logs do not reach me.
  • Chief status panel: when you open the "Progress and technical summary" panel on the home page, your browser sends a request to GitHub's public API (api.github.com) to fetch the project's last update date. If you don't open the panel, no request is sent.

The Sivas 5-8 game

Your saved game and sound preference are kept only in your own browser's local storage (localStorage) and are never sent to a server. The game's "Yeni oyun" (New game) button deletes the save; clearing your browser's site data deletes everything.

Your rights

Under Turkey's data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698, Article 11) and, if you are in Europe, the GDPR, you have the right to know what data I hold about you and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Just write to me through the contact form; the only data I hold is the messages you have sent me.

Reporting a security vulnerability

If you have found a security vulnerability on this site or in my public projects on GitHub (e.g. Chief-Sef), please tell me first:

  • Use the contact form on the home page and start your message with "Security".
  • Briefly describe the steps to reproduce the issue and its impact.
  • Do not access other people's data, run tests that could disrupt the service (e.g. load or DoS tests), or attempt social engineering.

I will read your report and reply as soon as I can. If you like, I will credit you by name when the fix is published. Machine-readable contact information: /.well-known/security.txt.
